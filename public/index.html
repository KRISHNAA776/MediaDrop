Supported Platforms

Each platform is handled by its own adapter that declares what is permitted. Metadata uses the platform's official oEmbed endpoint.

  • YouTube, Vimeo, X, TikTok, Reddit: link analysis (title, creator, thumbnail) and a link to the original. No direct download.
  • Facebook, Instagram: same, but Meta requires an approved app token (META_OEMBED_TOKEN). Without it, analysis is unavailable.
  • Direct media files (.mp4, .webm, .mp3, .m4a …): files you own or are authorized to use can be downloaded as the original, unmodified file.

How It Works

  1. Paste a link and press Analyze Link.
  2. The server validates the URL, identifies the platform and checks what that platform's adapter permits.
  3. You see only verified information. Formats are never invented or upscaled.
  4. If a download is permitted, the button starts a real download of the original file. Otherwise you get the official link.

FAQ

Why can't I download from YouTube or Instagram?

Those platforms do not permit downloads through unofficial tools. MediaDrop does not bypass logins, DRM, private accounts or rate limits.

Do you convert audio?

Not in this version. Only original files are offered, and we never label a file with a higher quality than the source.

How long are download links valid?

10 minutes. Nothing is stored on the server; files stream straight from the source.

Do you need my social media login?

Never. Do not share passwords, cookies or tokens with anyone.
